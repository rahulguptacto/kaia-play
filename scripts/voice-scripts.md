# Kaia's World — Voice Scripts

## Voice Design Principles
- Talk TO Kaia like a warm, patient caregiver (not AT her)
- Every phrase should feel like a conversation, not a command
- Use her name frequently — toddlers light up hearing their name
- Vary energy levels: excited for praise, gentle for instructions, playful for games
- Include sound effects in speech (stretching words, singsongy tone)
- Phrases should be 3-8 seconds, not 1-word clips

## ElevenLabs Voice Selection
- **Primary (Female):** Warm, slightly higher pitch, enthusiastic but not manic. "Rachel" or "Bella" voice.
- **Secondary (Male):** Friendly, calm, for DJ Bunny and variety. "Adam" or "Josh" voice.
- **Personal recordings (you/wife):** "Hi Kaia I love you", "Good night Kaia", key praise phrases
